In this test you find the hidden sides of an object. You are shown a stack of blocks, all the same size. You can see the front and the top, but not the sides facing away from you or the bottom. Use your imagination to picture what the hidden sides look like.

Example

The answer for side A is iii, for side B it is i, and for side C it is v.

You have 5 minutes to answer as many questions as you can. Type the numeral shown, such as iii, ii or i, in lowercase letters.
