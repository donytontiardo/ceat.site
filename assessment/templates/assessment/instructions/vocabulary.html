This test looks at how well you understand words. You are given a word and four alternatives: choose the one closest in meaning to the word.

Example

Possess

A) Own
B) Pay
C) Surrender
D) Release

The answer is A, Own.

You have 5 minutes. Either you know a word or you don't, so there is little point in spending long on any one question. Choose the right answer from the choices given.
