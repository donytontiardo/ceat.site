This test asks a range of questions, from simple sums to longer calculations, with decimals, percentages and fractions. You may not use a calculator.

Example

What is 1/5 as a decimal?

Answer: 0.20

Type your answer in the answer box, following these rules:

1. Write decimals with a point (.), not a comma (,), as in the example.

2. Write decimals with AT LEAST two (2) digits after the point, as in the example.

3. Leave out symbols you don't need, such as $, £, € and %.

4. If a question asks for a fraction, answer with a fraction, such as 1/4, 2/3 or 5/5.

5. Otherwise, answer with a decimal.
