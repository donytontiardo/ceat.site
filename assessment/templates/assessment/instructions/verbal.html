This test looks at how well you draw logical conclusions from the information you are given. There is always enough information to reach the answer, so read carefully.

Example

Newport is further west than Flatpeak, though not as far west as Daybridge.

Which town is furthest east?

A) Newport
B) Flatpeak
C) Daybridge

The answer is B, Flatpeak. Daybridge is furthest west and Newport comes second, which leaves Flatpeak furthest east.

You have 10 minutes to answer as many questions as you can. Choose the best answer from the choices given.
