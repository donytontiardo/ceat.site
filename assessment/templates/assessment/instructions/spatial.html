This test explores how easily you can picture objects turned around in space. You are shown one object at the top and five numbered objects below it. Decide for each one whether it is the same object as the one at the top.

An object is the same if it has only been turned or flipped over. It is different if its proportions or any of its parts have changed.

Example

The answers are: 1 = no, 2 = no, 3 = yes, 4 = yes, 5 = no.

You have 10 minutes to answer as many questions as you can. Choose Yes or No for each figure.
